Hello Marlies

Today I'm writing to you personally – with a message that means a lot to me. Please take a moment for it.

The Academy is a project of the heart – and projects of the heart ask a lot. Behind every tutorial and every choreography there are countless hours of work. That workload has grown far beyond what we imagined at the start. For Johnny – alongside his many engagements as a choreographer and the need to take good care of his health – it became almost impossible to carry. And at the same time, in our conversations, we felt that our visions for this path were no longer quite the same.

Sometimes the most honest and most loving step is to acknowledge that together.

That is exactly what we did: Gianmarco «Johnny» Rossato and the COUNTRY HIGH Dance Academy are going separate ways – on good terms, with respect and with great gratitude for the time we shared.

thank you, from the heart

Dear Johnny, thank you from the bottom of my heart. For your time, your energy, your passion – and for everything we experienced and shared far beyond dancing. Those moments stay forever. I'm grateful for the road we walked together. Take good care of yourself.

Your Kim
FOR ALL THE UNFORGETTABLE MOMENTS WE SHARED

With this farewell we also close a chapter that belonged to those first years: we are shutting down our current Academy platform on 21 August 2026. Not because the idea is over – but because what comes next is rethought from the ground up. We'll tell you in good time, and personally, what happens with your current access. Nobody is forgotten here.

the best is yet to come
The Academy is coming back

Late 2026, a new chapter begins: the COUNTRY HIGH Dance Academy is coming back – rebuilt, FOR YOU. We took all your feedback and wishes from the community seriously – they flow straight into the new offering.

This is how we're building the place where line dancers truly grow. Three things we can already share:

01You follow a clear path – with a structure that really moves you forward.
02It meets you exactly where you are – and takes you further, step by step.
03What you learn with us stays yours – with no expiry date.

Everything else stays our secret until then. 🤠
